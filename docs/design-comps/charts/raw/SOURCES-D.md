## Map outlines (not data)

`packages/shared/src/charts/geo/world.ts` is generated by `scripts/chart-world.py` from Natural Earth
5.1.1, Admin 0 countries, point of view India (`ne_10m_admin_0_countries_ind`,
https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector). Natural Earth: "All versions of Natural Earth raster
+ vector map data found on this website are in the public domain"
(https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/). The India view draws Jammu and Kashmir
(including Gilgit-Baltistan and Aksai Chin) and Arunachal Pradesh within India, as India depicts its
boundaries; the default Natural Earth file draws the de facto lines. A test checks points in Gilgit,
Aksai Chin and Tawang fall inside India and not inside Pakistan or China. The India tile grid
(`geo/india.ts`) draws no boundaries at all.

## Facts in the comps' notes, checked against the rows

- Fertility, 2024: 119 of 217 countries below 2.1; India 1.96.
- Internet, 2024: India 64.9%; the World Bank's world figure 71.2%.
- Gold, real: deepest fall 63% from the 1980 peak to the 1999 low; back above that peak in 2009.
- Gold, monthly 2016–2025: 69 of 120 months up; the longest run of rises six months, January to June 2025.
- Delhi calendar, 2024: no day of January above its normal high; May 25 of 31 days and June 25 of 30 above.
- Delhi heatmap: since 2000, May and June highs at or below their 1961–1990 averages in 73% and 69% of years; August above in 65%.
