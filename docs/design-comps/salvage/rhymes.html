Rhymes1865 and 2025

Cheaper power, more burning

The same goes for words. Cheap prose has given us a great deal more writing, and roughly the same amount worth reading. B.

1865

It is wholly a confusion of ideas to suppose that the economical use of fuel is equivalent to a diminished consumption. The very contrary is the truth.

W. Stanley Jevons, The Coal Question, chapter VII

then

Swap coal for computation. Jevons argued that a more efficient steam engine would raise Britain's coal use, because cheaper power found new work to do. The same argument is now made, almost word for word, about cheaper AI models.

2025

Jevons paradox strikes again!

Satya Nadella, on X, January 2025, after a cheap Chinese model rattled markets
