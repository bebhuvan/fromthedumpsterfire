In Praise of the Dilettante

Why it's here

I love the word dilettante. I think I fell in love with it as soon as I discovered it, because I consider myself a textbook case. Not the textbook definition, mind you, which is almost entirely negative. Merriam-Webster defines a dilettante as “a person having a superficial interest in an art or a branch of knowledge.”

The moment I read those definitions, I wanted to reclaim the word, because it captured an approach to intellectual life I’ve had for a long time.
