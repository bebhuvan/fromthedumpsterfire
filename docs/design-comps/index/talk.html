You and Your Research

Richard Hamming, a talk at Bellcore, 7 March 1986, 60 minutes

  1. Bellcore,
    7 March 1986    From the transcript.
  2. Hamming

    If you do not work on an important problem, it's unlikely you'll do important work.

  3. B.

    I listened to this on the drive back from Mysore. The bit that stuck is the Friday afternoons: he kept them only for great thoughts. And the line about important problems. If you don't work on important problems, you won't do important work. Everyone building something with AI right now should hear this, because it's so easy to be busy.

  4. Note

    Hamming gave this talk at Bellcore on 7 March 1986. Transcript header

  5. Note

    He spent thirty years at Bell Labs, from 1946 to 1976, and for a time shared an office with Claude Shannon. Wikipedia: Richard Hamming
