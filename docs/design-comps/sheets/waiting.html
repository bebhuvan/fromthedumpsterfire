Haul 001 is being gathered

From the Dumpster Fire is a magazine of things worth pulling out of the internet and the archive before they burn. It arrives in Hauls, when there is enough worth sending.

What a Haul holds

Six to twenty objects, each with a note on why it is here. Every kind of thing gets the printed form it has always had, so you know what you are holding before you read a word.

  • Essays and postsset as a magazine opening
  • Talks, videos and podcastsset as a transcript
  • Papersset as a journal offprint
  • Poems and passagesset alone on a broadside
  • Rhymesthen and now on facing pages
  • Rabbit holesfiled on a catalogue card

Read how it's made, or follow the RSS feed to hear when it lands.

The editor chooses and annotates every piece, mostly by voice. Language models read, cross-link and fact-check. Their notes are always set in this blue sans, so you can tell them apart.

Things worth salvaging from the internet and the archive. The serif is the editor; the blue sans is the machine.
