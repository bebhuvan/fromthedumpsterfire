You and Your Research

Richard Hamming, a talk to researchers at Bellcore

Bellcore
7 March 1986The Hamming lecture, about an hour long. The line below is what the editor kept.

HAMMINGIf you do not work on an important problem, it's unlikely you'll do important work.

B.I listened to this on the drive back from Mysore. The bit that stuck is the Friday afternoons: he kept them only for great thoughts. And the line about important problems. If you don't work on important problems, you won't do important work. Everyone building something with AI right now should hear this, because it's so easy to be busy.

NoteHamming gave this talk at Bellcore on 7 March 1986. Transcript header

NoteHe spent thirty years at Bell Labs, from 1946 to 1976, and for a time shared an office with Claude Shannon. Wikipedia: Richard Hamming
