Haul 001 is being gathered

A magazine of things worth pulling out of the internet and the archive before they burn. It arrives in Hauls, when there is enough worth sending.

What a Haul holds

  • Essays, set as a magazine opens one.
  • Papers, as offprints: abstract first, sections numbered.
  • Talks and podcasts, as transcripts, speakers in the margin.
  • Poems, as broadsides: one sheet, nothing else on it.
  • Rhymes, as facing pages: then on the left, now on the right.
  • Rabbit holes, as encyclopedia entries with their cross-references.

Until then

The first one is not out yet. Read how it's made, or follow the RSS feed to hear when it lands.

Chosen and annotated by one editor, mostly by voice. Language models read, cross-link and check; anything they write is set in this blue.
